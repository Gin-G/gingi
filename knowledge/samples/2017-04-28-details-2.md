Business Card Title / Posting Title Technical Program Manager - SolidFire
Requisition Number 42017BR
Recruiter Morgan Ganz 057123
Job Summary Job Summary:

We are looking for an experienced Software Program Manager who excels at
delivering high quality, on-time software products for our state-of-the-art
cloud storage solution. The ideal candidate will have a blend of experience
in formal program management, strong technical depth and excellent
communication skills, with a desire to make themselves and the team better.
Additionally, the Product Release Manager will have experience with
multiple software development lifecycle models (waterfall, iterative,
agile, etc.) and will have implemented various aspects of each methodology
as is appropriate to the situation and for the team.
Posting Location USA - Colorado - Boulder
USA - Colorado - Denver
Job Requirements The Software Program Manager drives engineering activities
throughout the product lifecycle to deliver high quality program releases
on schedule. More specific responsibilities include:

Collaborate with the engineering teams to develop the detailed work
breakdown schedules for complex software features
Track engineering progress against release schedules, developing
appropriate metrics to ensure early and often communication for the status
of the release
Identify risks and mitigation plans that maintain schedule integrity
Actively drive the engineering teams to ensure that product quality is
maintained throughout the SolidFire product lifecycle, including
requirements traceability, test suite tracking and defect triage
Work with the Build/Release engineer to facilitate continuous
integration/delivery within the release
Drive innovation and integration of new software development processes and
quality initiatives into projects and activities
Education and Experience Qualified candidates must have a track record of
successfully managing software features and releases from concept to new
product introduction. Additional requirements include:

5+ years experience as a technical program manager with a track record of
delivering complex software features and releases on time, with high
quality
Strong leadership and analytical skills in combination with excellent
written and verbal communication abilities. The candidate must be
self-driven and an expert at multi-tasking
Experience managing using various software development lifecycle models.
Big plus if you have migrated teams from a more traditional waterfall
methodology to agile
Experience with build/release methodologies and best practices
e.g.continuous integration, as well as knowledge of branch management
policies
Ability to understand the technical details of the software your teams are
working on. You should be able to assess risk, challenge estimates,
identify dependencies and ultimately guide the team to the optimal schedule
B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Computer Science or other related field or
equivalent
Experience as a technical contributor (software developer, quality
engineering, etc.) a plus
Experience working on system level software in the data storage industry is
a plus
