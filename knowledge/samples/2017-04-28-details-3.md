Job Summary Job Summary:

We are looking for an experienced Program Management Lead who excels at
delivering high quality, on-time software products for our state-of-the-art
cloud storage solution and has experience leading a small team of program
managers. This is a player/coach role where you will be equally comfortable
actively managing programs as well as leading the team.

The ideal candidate will have a blend of experience in formal program
management, strong technical depth and excellent communication skills, with
a desire to make the team better. Ideally the Program Management Lead will
also have experience transforming teams to agile.

Opportunities to Make an Impact:
The Program Management Lead drives engineering activities throughout the
product lifecycle to deliver high quality program releases on schedule.
More specific responsibilities include:

Player
Collaborate with engineering to develop detailed plans for complex software
features
Track engineering progress against release schedules
Identify risks and mitigation plans that maintain schedule integrity
Measure and drive high product quality
Coach
Lead the program management team to continuously improve through a
combination best practices, consistency and innovation
Provide expertise and leadership in migration to agile development
practices
Act as a representative for the team across Solidfire engineering and the
broader NetApp
Career management and mentorship of program management team
Posting Location USA - Colorado - Boulder
Job Requirements Qualified candidates must have a track record of
successfully managing software features and releases from concept to new
product introduction. Additional requirements include:

5+ years experience as a technical program manager with a track record of
delivering complex software features and releases on time, with high
quality
Strong leadership and analytical skills in combination with excellent
written and verbal communication abilities
2+ years experience as a lead or manager
Experience with agile methodologies e.g. Scrum, SAFe. Bonus if you have
migrated teams from a more traditional waterfall methodology to agile
Ability to understand the technical details of the software your teams are
working on. You should be able to assess risk, challenge estimates,
identify dependencies and ultimately guide the team to the optimal schedule
Education and Experience B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Computer Science or other
related field or equivalent
