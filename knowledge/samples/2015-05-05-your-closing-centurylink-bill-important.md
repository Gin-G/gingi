We want to make sure you understand your Closing bill.

                        We're sorry to lose you as a CenturyLink customer. We want you to know what to expect next.

                        Your closing bill(s) will be affected by the timing of your disconnection of service.

                        Since most of our services are billed in advance, you may see credits for days between your disconnection of service and the end of your billing cycle.

                        For services that are billed based on usage, such as some long distance plans, it's possible some charges will not be included on your next bill. You will receive a revised closing bill in these situations.

                        An easy way to avoid additional charges to your closing bill

                        If you have leased equipment, one easy way to avoid additional charges to your account is to return the equipment as soon as possible. If CenturyLink receives your returned equipment before your closing bill is created, then you won't have to worry about incurring new charges for that unreturned equipment.

                       If you have any questions regarding the return of leased equipment (i.e. modems, routers, etc), please chat with us online or simply reply to this email.
