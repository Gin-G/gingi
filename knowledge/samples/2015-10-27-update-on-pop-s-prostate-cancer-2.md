Hi everyone,     Pop wanted everyone to get an update as soon as possible and the bottom line is:  all will be well. I've tried to send this to as many of the family as possible but undoubtedly have missed some so please forward this on as needed. Meme, Pop, Lisa and I met with the oncologist yesterday. The good news is that Pop's cancer is treatable and controllable and he should live on for many years, God willing. The bad news is that the prostate cancer has spread to several of his bones. Fortunately it hasn't spread anywhere else. So, surgery isn't needed because the point of surgery is to remove the cancer before it spreads.  Similarly, radiation therapy isn't indicated at this time and may never be needed. What he does need is a shot every 3-6 months to stop testosterone production because the cancer "feeds" on testosterone. However, keep in mind that if we lived in a less-developed country the only other way to stop testosterone production would be.....castration (yikes). So Pop is definitely looking on the bright side of this. He got his first shot yesterday. Overall,  his situation is  very parallel to Meme's situation where the cancer is there but isn't growing and isn't likely to cause any major issues. Pop also is likely to do an 18 week round of chemo with Taxol (which Meme got after several other rounds of nastiness and hated.... but it's a different situation with Pop and the oncologist says it is usually very well tolerated).The Taxol and the Lupron should decrease the now-evident but previously unrecognized bony aches that Pop has had intermittently for a while and our probably due to the cancer in the bone. He will need to be careful about maintaining his bone strength, taking calcium supplements and doing weight- bearing exercises. Also he will need to be careful when skiing to stay safe and not fall. There are some additional side effects but hopefully none too bad. Oh yes,  the other  bad news is that his golf game probably is going  to get better and those of us who haven't been able to beat him have likely missed our window of opportunity! Love all of you, Dan
