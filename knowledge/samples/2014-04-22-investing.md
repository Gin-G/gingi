It mostly has to be a feeling. The best bet is to google a topic you are
interested in followed by stock, investing, or publicly traded after it in
the search bar. Do some research on the companies that come up and form
your own opinion. You can follow companies for a bit and see how your
feelings play out before you find a company you really would put your money
behind and expect something in return.
