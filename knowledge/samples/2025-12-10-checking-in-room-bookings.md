*Payment Options:*

   1. *Full Payment:*
   Full payment of the hotel price at the time of booking.
   2. *Love Away Payment Plan:*
   A $100 deposit is required at the time of booking. The date your
   reservation is confirmed will determine your subsequent payment schedule.
   Every 30 days, the credit card on file will be automatically charged $100.
   This will continue monthly until the final balance is due. Final payment is
   due *14 days before arrival*.
   3. *Deferred Payment Plan:*
   A deposit equivalent to 2 nights is required at the time of booking.
   Final payment is due*14 days before arrival*.
