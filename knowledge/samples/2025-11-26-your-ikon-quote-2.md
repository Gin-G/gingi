To redeem your Ikon Pass, follow these steps:

  *   Copy/Paste https://account.ikonpass.com/en/promocode into your browser.
  *   Type in code exactly as you see it listed in the Confirmation section of this document. (All ‘0’ are always zeros. Letters will auto fill as capitals.) Click “Apply.”
  *   Your code, pass type and age category will be displayed with “FREE.” If the pass type is correct, click “Add To Cart.”
  *   You can add family members’ codes to your cart as well. Enter each code in the “Add Promo Code” field and click “Apply” and then “Add To Cart” after each.
  *   Once all codes are entered, click “View Cart” at bottom of page.
  *   Confirm each pass shows as “FREE.” Your total in the cart should read $0.00. At this step, you have the option to add Spot Injury Insurance to your pass for an additional $99 per pass, paid at the time of purchase. Please review all additional details of Spot coverage. When ready, click “Proceed To Checkout.”
  *   Log In or Create an account
  *   Assign each pass to each family member or yourself. Click “Continue.”
  *   Read through the Waiver & Release of Liability and check the box “I Agree” and “NEXT” at the bottom of the page.
  *   Click “I Accept the Access Terms” should it apply.
  *   Confirm or add Shipping Address and Phone Number. Click “Continue.”
  *   Review information, including your shipping address. Click
“I agree to Terms and Policy.” Then click “Purchase Now” to finish your transaction.
  *   You will see a confirmation with an order number.
  *   Your Ikon pass will be mailed before the start of the 2025/26 season if you have signed the waiver, uploaded a photo, and have a valid mailing address on your account.
  *   Note: If you wish to add Spot pass protection or injury insurance to your pass, please save a credit card to your Ikon Pass account, then email your insurance request to mountainambassador@alterramtnco.com<mailto:mountainambassador@alterramtnco.com> within 3 days of code redemption.
