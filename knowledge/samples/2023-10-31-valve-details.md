Just taking some notes on these pneumatic parts:

CMKM-435 (Cylinder Mount Valve Subplate)
https://store.livhaven.com/cmkm-435-versa-pneumatic-valve-subplate

KSP-4312-40 (Pneumatic Directional Valve)
https://www.mrostop.com/ksp-4312-40-versa-pneumatic-directional-valve

Those two look to be in stock. I'm still not 100% on this subplate. I don't
think it gets a plate on top. I can't tell for sure, there are no great
engineering diagrams to look at. I think the valve was sticking and got
stuck permanently but maybe the subplate was OK? The relief screws are
there to fine-tune the airflow into the cylinder, allowing better control
of the speed the cylinder extends and retracts. Open them up and let a
little extra air out to slow things down.

Getting them both new wouldn't be a terrible idea. If the other subplate is
still good it's a spare part. The only moving part is in the directional
valve which is what they would have been trying to move when they banged on
the whole thing.
