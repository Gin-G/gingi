Hello Nick !
Welcome to the next steps of your LHH program! I know it may seem like a lot but putting together a simple, yet comprehensive action plan will help you in the next steps of your career journey.
My name is Alison and I am your Career Consultant. My role is to provide recommendations for pathways of success for you. This is what it will look like:

 *  I will email you weekly with recommendations of Live Events, Career Resources, and Networking Opportunities.
 *  Soon, I will also start leading “Open Office Hours” and 30-minute Group Workshops that will showcase educational and career support. Topics will include How do I use my CRN, Top 5 reasons to have a LinkedIn Profile, How to Create an Effective Action Plan, etc.

So here are your first steps towards success:

 *  When you log in to your Career Resource Network (CRN), be sure to click on the top of the screen “Get Started on My CRN”. You will select your roadmap and goals; as well as have the opportunity to watch a getting started video. This video will showcase many of the tools and resources within the CRN.
 *  Be the lookout for invitations to Open Forum sessions where you can meet with career experts and get support where you need it most.
 *  If you have not logged onto your Career Resources Network, please do so (crn.lhh.com - credentials were in your first email from LHH).
 *  Click on "Ella", our Digital Job Search Agent, and set your ideal job search preferences so that we can begin sourcing job leads for you right away.
 *  And lastly, feel free to connect with me on LinkedIn HERE.

LHH is passionate about the value we deliver to our candidates, and I am dedicated to your success and look forward to working with you!
With best regards,
