Summary of Tuition Classification Regulations

These tuition classification regulations are explained in greater detail

2. Domicile is defined as your true, fixed, and permanent home and place of
habitation. Domicile is a legal characteristic that everyone has, and you
can have only one domicile at any one time. Your domicile is your legal,
primary residence.

3. If your parents are not Colorado domiciliaries, you must be a qualified
person to begin the one-year domiciliary period. A qualified person is
someone who is either: at least 22 years old, or married, or emancipated,
or a graduate student. Persons who are not citizens of the United States
should read the section regarding aliens for additional information.
