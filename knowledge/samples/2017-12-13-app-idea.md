Hey Dude,
I had an idea while falling asleep the other night. Going to run the basics
through you, see what you think.

At the core, all it is will be is an app where you answer yes or no to
questions.

Ex.

Do you give money to homeless people?

                     Yes           No

Fairly simple, but why would people want to use it, or what do they get for
using it?

I have a few ideas on that but it's the main piece to start with. I imagine
each question getting assigned a "character trait." So with the example
above we could say that is an empathy question. A yes answer adds +1 to
your empathy score, no is -1. Do you workout? Yes +1 to health, etc...
Questions could have more than one associated character trait.

So we start by telling the user answer 5 questions and we will tell you
your character traits. Answer more questions and we will build a better
picture of your character traits. Expand this in to creating your own
questions, sharing questions on social media, and eventually using it for
advertising purposes or product recommendations/placement. There's also
room to make it a self help app. Like you have a -15 Empathy score, would
you like to try and change that? Here's some ways to do that.

That's kind of my initial thoughts on it all. There is plenty to expand on
if you do think there's potential.
