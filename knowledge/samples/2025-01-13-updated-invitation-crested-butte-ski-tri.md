I vote for being close to the mountain. There's a free bus into town, and
I'd prefer to take that without ski gear than work out the logistics of
getting to the mountain with gear. It runs every 15 minutes from 7:10 AM
until midnight and the stop is 5 minutes walking distance from the current
place.

that's OK too. It does sleep 8, but that's between 4 beds and a pull-out
couch. I don't mind paying a little more for some space. There's also
nothing better after a hard day of skiing than hitting the hot tub so +1
for that.

I'm open to everyone else's opinion but will say being closer to the
mountain for me is the way to go and maximizes our time on the slopes.
