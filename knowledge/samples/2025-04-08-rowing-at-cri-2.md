Hi Nick,

Thanks for clarifying the circumstances! I am available Tuesday 7/1 through
Thursday 7/3 at 10:30am, if any of those work for you? 1.5 hours sounds
good. You don't need to bring any shoes -- we have tons of sizes to clip in
and out of the boat. Also, unless you're interested in more than one
outing, it's unlikely you'll be in a tippy racing boat that has shoes. The
wider beginner boats have straps instead (used with socks).

Before you come, please watch the USRowing safety video
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rx5SUe_RdgQ>, and I'd also recommend
watching this video
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qg8Rb60BmJQ&t=26s> and this
video from 0:17 to 1:00 <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKEk0lASjwI> to
see what really good sculling looks like. Bring a spare pair of clothes in
case you fall in or get wet, and if you bring a water bottle it's best to
bring a soft plastic one, or you can cover a harder bottle with a sock to
protect the boat.

Serena
