*Can you seal or expunge a DUI/DWAI conviction in Colorado?*

If you are found guilty or plead guilty to these crimes as misdemeanors or
felonies in Colorado as an adult, you will not have legal recourse to
expunge or seal the conviction from your permanent criminal or motor
vehicle record, even with the best DUI attorneys. While defendants and
their legal counsel can expunge some criminal offenses, such as minor drug
convictions, misdemeanors and certain felonies, in all cases, DUI/DWAI
convictions are only expunged or sealed for minors
<http://www.orrlaw.com/resources/traffic-offenses-resources/traffic-offense-statutes/minor-in-possession/>
and
only if the case is prosecuted in a juvenile court.
