Dear Pop,
I got the opportunity to speak in front of everyone in my division on
Tuesday, probably a little less than 400 people, and I did a great job
getting my messages across. It was a little intense having everyones focus
on me, but it is more exposure to the great work I always deliver. I've
been getting a lot more exposure at work lately, and it is putting me in a
great place to really gain an enormous amount of experience while making a
noticeable impact in our company.

Of course I'd rather be doing other things than work most of the time and I
still take advantage of every opportunity I've got. I shot a 46 at golf
league last week. It was my best score this season, but I am working on
improving my game a lot. I started taking lessons, I can tell they are
making a difference, and got out for 36 holes last week.

I'm going to get Carolyn some lessons at the local course you and I played
at together, but she's been busy with other things. She has PA school
applications in at 6 schools, 3 in Colorado, and 3 in New England. It's a
competitive field these days, but she won't give up and of course I am
confident in her ability to get in and succeed, and will continue to
support her career growth.

We went to a wedding this Saturday, and celebrated our 2nd wedding
anniversary on Sunday. We had a great time getting there through Rocky
Mountain national park, a blast dancing and enjoying the wedding together,
followed up by kayaking at Grand Lake on our anniversary. It's a blessing
to have someone in my life who loves me as much as she does, not only
supporting me to get out and enjoy life, but supports me when things don't
go well. We will always love and support each other to accomplish our goals
and be happy.

All this greatness in my life wouldn't have been capable without a great
man providing guidance to us all. When I was at my lowest point, lacking
confidence in myself trying to get back in to school, you provided the
voice of reason and logic that enabled me to start taking a different view
on life. I read your response to my 1st draft letter to get back in to
school and haven't looked at myself the same way since. From that point on
my life has gotten exponentially better, and I will never forget the
encouragement you gave me when I needed it most.

Thank you for everything. You do so much for your family. We know you as
the man who provides support, inspiration, humor, and countless hours of
fun all while being the ultimate voice of reason. You do it the right way,
and I will do it the right way with my family.

I am so happy and optimistic about my future. I'm really excited to teach
our kids to play golf, ski, encourage them to always increase there
knowledge, and have fun with life while knowing they have all our support.
I will provide support to people in our family the way you always have as
well. We have all been setup to succeed but of course sometimes we all need
a little extra help and encouragement. I have and always will make you
proud by implementing so many of the life lessons you have taught us all.
You are Pop, and I love you so so very much.
