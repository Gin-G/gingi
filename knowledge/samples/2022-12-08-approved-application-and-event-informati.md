Approved Application and Event Information

                You have received an update from Moms and Mutts Colorado Rescue for Pregnant & Nursing Dogs.

                 regarding your Adoption Application.

                Moms and Mutts Colorado Rescue for Pregnant & Nursing Dogs has written you the following note:

Hello,

I have just finished reviewing your application and your home seems like the perfect place for one of our dogs to call its own. We host our adoptions at our office in Englewood, CO every Friday and Saturday. We have also instituted an appointment schedule for each event. Due to limited space, the dogs attending the events are split into two categories: a) Deposit Only and b) Open Adoption. The criteria we use for each group of dogs is listed below. Only approved adopters are allowed to schedule appointments for the events

Deposit only dogs/puppies are the newly listed puppies, puppies who have less than 2 vaccinations, and adult dogs. Adult dogs do not do well in event environments so we do our best to accommodate meet and greets outside of event times to reduce their stress. Open event dogs/puppies are puppies with 2 or more vaccinations, older puppies, and puppies who have had a difficult time finding an adopter. Deposits can be placed on dogs in either category, and those dogs will be reserved for their potential adopters. The open adoption event sign up list can be located on our Facebook page: https://www.facebook.com/MAMCORescue. You may also email us at info@mamcorescue.org to receive the link to the events.

We will respond to requests for adoption deposits in the order they are received via email. Please write DEPOSIT and the dog's name in the subject line; let us know you have an approved application and provide the best number to reach you on. Sending multiple emails moves your name further down the list, and clogs our inbox, so please avoid this if possible.

If a dog no longer shows up on our website, that means the dog has a deposit and is no longer available. https://mamcorescue.org/adoptable-dogs/ We DO NOT have an online deposit system. DO NOT send money via Petstablished, Paypal, or any other way.

While deposits are non refundable, they are transferable to any available dog in our rescue for 6 months. The deposit to hold a dog is a $250 non-refundable deposit which is applied to the adoption fee. The remainder of the adoption fee will be due at the time the adoption is finalized. You can find more information on our adoption fees here: https://mamcorescue.org/adoption-fees/; Deposits will be taken in the order they are received via our email: info@mamcorescue.org.

Please plan to bring a check for $300 for us to hold on file. This check is for the spay/neuter deposit to ensure that the procedure is completed by the time the dog is 6 months old, and will NOT be cashed unless you fail to provide us with proof of spay or neuter. We are no longer accepting credit cards for spay and neuter deposits and are requiring all adopters to bring a check. Please plan accordingly.

Please note: When a dog no longer appears on the website, that means that he or she has been reserved via deposit. We appreciate your patience during this time.

Sincerely,

Aron Jones and the MAMCO Staff
