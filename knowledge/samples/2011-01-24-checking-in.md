Hey Meme and Pop,

      Just thought I'd shoot you an e-mail to let you know how the first two
weeks of classes have been going so far.  I have 5 classes in total and most
just boil down to basic mechanical engineering knowledge classes.  I have
Dynamics, a glorified version of physics that seems like it will be a very
good class.  The professor is a smart guy and I like the way he delivers his
lecture.  We get a printout of the notes before he goes through them with
examples so we can follow along and add important bits here and there while
still being able to focus on why things are one and not the other,
 Thermodynamics 2 is just standard ME stuff.  It's been going through
different cycles and determining work inputs and outputs along with change
in energy.  Heat Transfer is along the same lines, you can guess from the
name we learn about heat transfer in different situations.  The professor is
Asian and doesn't speak english very well but the material is presented well
and I was able to work my way through the first homework without any
troubles. Data Analysis so far has been mean, median, mode stuff with some
probability.  Just a lot of number crunching in the end and deciphering word
problems so as long as I do all the work it should be no problem.  The last
class I'm taking is going to be my favorite, component design.  I work with
a group all semester and we are faced with the challenged in designing and
building a 1 person vehicle, like a bike, that is powered by a cordless
drill.  We have to do financial and technical, stress and gearing, etc.,
analysis along the way and the final report will be fairly detailed.  I'm
just looking forward to getting in to the machine shop and building some
thing based on force analyses we've already done.  All in all it will be a
challenging, most likely the hardest so far and overall, but interesting
semester.
