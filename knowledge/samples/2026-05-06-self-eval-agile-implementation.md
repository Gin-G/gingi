I served as the Technical Product Owner for both the GDEX and CIRRUS teams this fiscal year, running standup meetings, sprint planning, retrospectives, and demonstrations on a regular cadence. The average time to complete tickets for these projects has dropped through the year. For GDEX we went from 75 days average to 23, and CIRRUS went from 72 to 24. We delivered well over 5 use cases between both projects. The Agile methodology put in place drove the GDEX migration to CIRRUS to a successful production cutover in September 2025. The CIRRUS site showcases over 10 live examples and we connected runners to over 24 GitHub repositories. I've also collected direct user feedback from well over 10 users of GDEX and CIRRUS this year to create new user stories and drive prioritization. Over the course of the year I onboarded new team members to the GDEX Agile process. Based on input from the team during retrospectives we switched from scrum to kanban to better fit the teams work as well as many other small tweaks to constantly improve the process.
