I just got a chance to look over the assessment. I think it looks really
good and targets pattern recognition, problem solving, as well as touching
on some basic coding principles. I work with a lot of development people
but don't work on it a lot myself, although I do have some familiarity with

and based of the students results you would be able to make a very informed
decision on who is capable and who is not. I think it might be nice to add
in a more detailed if/else statement question. From the small amount of

reach out with mroe questions, especially if you have any thing more
