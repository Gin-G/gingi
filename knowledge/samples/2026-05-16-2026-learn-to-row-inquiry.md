*Learn to Row*—

Our courses teach the basic rowing mechanics and essential boat management
skills. These programs will be primarily taught in sweep boats (one oar per
rower), with one session of sculling (two oars per rower) instruction also
available. Upon completion, you may join our Novice-coached programs or
even Club Squad in late summer and fall.

The cost of the sweep class is $150. The sculling class costs $100 and is
reserved for people who have completed the sweep class or those who already
know how to row sweep. Registration and payment are handled on
BoathouseConnecct.com.

*2026 June Learn to Row Schedule*Additional dates for July and August will

*Step 1: *Visit Boathouse Connect <https://boathouseconnect.com/> and
create an account. After your account is created, select “BCR - Boulder
Community Rowing” from the drop down on the page to request to join the
organization.

*Step 2:* If this is your first year with BCR, you will need to add a
payment method to cover the cost of the programs. You can do this by going
to Finances -> My Finances
<https://boathouseconnect.com/finances/myFinances> and adding a
debit/credit card.

*Step 3:* Once your account has been approved to join BCR, you can join the
programs of your choice by going to Groups -> My Groups
<https://boathouseconnect.com/groups/mygroups> and joining the group
related to your program.

*Step 4:* You can set your attendance plans for the coming month by going
to Practices -> My Attendance
<https://boathouseconnect.com/practices/attendance>. If you don’t see any
practices on this page, you may not be in the right groups. Go to Groups ->
My Groups <https://boathouseconnect.com/groups/mygroups> to join the
appropriate groups for the programs you are participating in.

*Step 5:* Sign up for a US Rowing membership
<https://membership.usrowing.org/> (basic level) and sign the associated
waiver. Update your Boathouse Connect account to include your membership
number: Go to My Waivers and Docs>My Waivers and Docs
<https://boathouseconnect.com/waivers/mywaivers>

*Please send a message to LearnToRow@boulderrowing.org
<LearnToRow@boulderrowing.org> if you have additional questions! *
