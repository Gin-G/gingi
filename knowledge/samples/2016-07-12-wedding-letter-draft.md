~ Acclimating for those of you who are traveling from out of state:

The elevation in Colorado is not something to take lightly. The airport is
located in Denver where the elevation is about 5,280 feet, in Boulder it is
about 5,430 feet, and at Arapahoe Basin, the wedding and lodging location,
it is 11,550 feet.

We recommend arriving as early as your schedule allows in order to ensure
adequate time to acclimate.

In order to avoid altitude sickness, your body requires plenty of water;
drink a lot of water the week prior to and during your trip.  Staying
hydrated and getting plenty of rest is key in managing the effects the
altitude has on your body.

There will be an Oxygen Bar at the wedding providing medical grade oxygen

In regards to alcohol consumption, here in Denver’s rarified air, golf
balls go 10% farther … and so do cocktails. Alcoholic drinks pack more of a
wallop here than at sea level. It is recommended that you go easy on the
alcohol, as its effects will feel stronger here.

~Attire: Mountain Formal/Dressy Casual

Outdoor Ceremony with an Indoor reception

We want our guests to be comfortable, dressed for the outdoors, and ready
to dance! For example, a cute sundress with flats or a summer suit/khakis
are some options. The weather in Colorado is unpredictable and it can rain,
hit 85°, and be windy all in one day, especially in August; we encourage
you to dress for the elements (sun/rain/wind/clouds). Because the weather
changes are so unpredictable, a sweater or a jacket is recommended and will
probably be useful for, at least, the chair lift escort back down the
mountain.
