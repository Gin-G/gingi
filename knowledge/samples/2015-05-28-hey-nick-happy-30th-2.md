It can't avoid obstacles and the top speed is only 25 MPH. My friend and I
have had some heated discussions about these. I don't think they are going
to take off and be huge due to a number of limitations such as battery life
and concern from the general population. They will be cool, niche, little
devices for certain people, but the general public won't have a huge
application for these. Add in the fact that the FAA is implementing drone
measures and some mountains, like Vail, don't allow the use of drones. My
buddy on the other hand thinks they are going to be huge with applications
ranging from what we see now to personal protection. I think we bet $500
that by 2020 there would be personal security applications that use drones.
I am on the side that there will not be.
