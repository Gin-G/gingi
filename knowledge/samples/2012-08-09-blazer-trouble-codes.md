Hi Nick, The codes are for running lean. Check for dirty Mass Air Flow
Sensor (clean) - Dirty Fuel injectors (Add Sea Foam Cleaner to gas tank )
Possible fuel filter (replace) Weak fuel pump (pressure should be 60 psi at
wide open throttle) Leaking vacuum from intake manifold (spray with water
and look for a change in the idle) Dirty air filter - Replace PO171 and
PO174 on Identifix program results. Let me know how you make out. Love Dad
