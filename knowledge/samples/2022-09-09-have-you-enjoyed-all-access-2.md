I thoroughly enjoy the benefits of all access. Opensnow.com is my homepage,
even in the summer, and I use it and the app religiously during ski season.
I've paid for the subscription every year since you went to a subscription
service and it's always worth it. I've been able to get in so many (always
wish it was more but hey you just predict the weather you don't control it)
great powder days because of the services offered through OpenSnow. When
you work M-F you gotta prepare to take those sick days a few days in
advance, make sure it comes together in the short term forecast, then I
usually set my alarm for even before the email alerts I have set come in to
check the snow stake web cams. I 100% am renewing.

I also appreciate the change last year in what is offered for free. Those 3
day forecasts are enough for most people to get out there when it dumps,
but the information provided is too valuable to be given out for free in my
opinion. I had seen the effects on wednesday powder days, heard the local
cursing Joel Gratz for longer lines to start the day, and I do think
limiting free content will maybe cut down on that. The die-hards, with the
real clinical powder sickness, will pay good money for the precise and
accurate information provided.

I sincerely thank everyone for the hard work that has gone into all this
over the years. I've had so many excellent days because of it.
