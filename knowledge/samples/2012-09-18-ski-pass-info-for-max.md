Hey Mom,

The website for this is at this address,

http://www.snow.com/epic-pass.aspx

At the top you will see a link that says the passes.  The two possible
choices are the Epic Pass and the Epic Local Pass.  The Epic Pass is
$680 and is unlimited at Vail, Beaver Creek, Keystone, Breckenridge
and Arapahoe Basin.  The Epic Local Pass is $530 and is unlimited at
Keystone, Breckenridge and Arapahoe Basin.  The local pass only gets
you 10 days to split between Vail and Beaver Creek.  If you ski at
those two places more than 10 times you need to buy a day pass for
$100.  The local pass is also blacked out at Vail and Beaver Creek for
the following dates - 11/23 – 11/24/2012, 12/26 – 12/31/2012,
1/19/2013, 2/16- 2/17/2013. I got the Epic Pass since I'm planning on
being here and skiing over Christmas and the local pass would not let
me ski at Vail during that time.  I'm also hoping to get in more than
10 days at Vail.  I always worry that I made the right decision by
getting the full pass.  Last year it didn't pay off, the previous year
I got over 20 days at Vail and it easily paid for itself.  Hopefully
we get some snow and I get in a bunch of good days.  Ask me if you
have any questions.  Love you,
