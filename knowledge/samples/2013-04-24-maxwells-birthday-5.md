So here is the recipe:

One round of Brie Cheese (remove rind w/Peeler) cut into small pieces
4-5 fresh tomatoes (cut up)
3-4 cloves ground garlic
a bunch of basil cut up
Bowtie Pasta (cooked)

Put olive oik (about 1/4 C) into a pasta bowl (you can serve the dish in

with a dishcloth and let sit all afternoon (I make it in the morning before

Add the cooked pasta to bowl and mix it up.  Serve individually and have
everyone add their choice of meat/scallop.....
